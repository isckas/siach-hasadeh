# Siach HaSadeh: v4 list

Things agreed for v4, after v3 is out. Nothing here is built yet.

## Fixes
- **Don't let devices darken the board.** Android TV boxes and some phones (Chrome's automatic dark mode for websites, Samsung Internet's dark mode) recolour pages that don't say they are light-only. On the shul TVs this turned the cream cards dark and the QR codes blank until Fully Kiosk's Dark Mode was turned off. Declare the pages light-only (`color-scheme: only light`, as a meta tag and in the CSS) on the website, the TV and the admin page, and check on an Android phone with dark mode on. Could go out earlier as a small v3 fix if the board agrees.
- **Friday night and Motzei Shabbat prayer notes.** The "Today" column follows the calendar date, so on Friday night it still shows Friday's notes ("ותן טל ומטר", "תחנון בשחרית בלבד"), and on Motzei Shabbat it leaves out "ותן טל ומטר" although Arvit includes it. They should follow the evening's prayers.
- **Rework the Shabbat board (TV).** Decide with Yossi (and the rav where it's halachic) before building, then design it with the design skill:
  - **What's shown.** Shabbat is when most people are in shul, so it's when the announcements, flyers and community news matter most, yet the still board shows none of them today. Make them the heart of the Shabbat board. Without slides changing, that means one still page laid out before Shabbat: the admins mark which announcements and flyers go on it, and it fits them all at once. If the rav allows slow rotation, that can be a setting. Also, the Shabbat times appear twice (the middle card and the "Today" column): show them once, which frees the space.
  - **What moves.** Today the still board has no clock and no slides, but the sun/moon marker on the top line still moves. Agree on the list of what may change on its own during Shabbat (nothing at all, or only pre-set things such as the marker or switching between Friday night and Shabbat day) and make it a setting. The biggest question is the **clock**: hidden on Shabbat today; ask the rav whether it may run (useful for sof zman Shema and knowing when tefillah starts), and offer it as a setting, off until he decides.
  - **When it starts.** Today it starts at sunset (about 40 minutes after candle lighting) and ends at Shabbat's end. Ask the rav whether it should start at candle lighting instead.
  - **QR codes on Shabbat.** Nobody scans a phone on Shabbat, so the still board could drop its QR codes and give the space to announcements.
- **Donation QR codes on the TV are too close together.** The two PayBox codes (donate to the shul, and join the Tzedakah Box group) sit one above the other in the same card, so a phone can pick up the wrong one. QR guidance: codes must never touch, the gap should be at least half a code's width, and each needs its own short label saying what it does. Decided: keep two codes, each going straight to its PayBox page (no in-between page, fewest taps), placed in two separate places on the screen, each with its own label.

## New features
- **Print the week's schedule.** A "Print the schedule" button on the website (and in the admin page) that makes one A4 page, in black and white and light on ink: this Shabbat (candle lighting, Friday night and Shabbat times, end of Shabbat, parasha), the coming week's prayer times, the shiurim with their days and times, and this week's events. It's built from the live data the moment someone presses it, so it's never out of date and nothing has to be made each week. The phone or computer's own print window then prints it or saves it as a PDF; that is also what renders Hebrew properly, which PDF-making libraries in the browser handle badly. Light ink means a white page, black text at normal weight, hairline dividers, and no filled boxes, backgrounds or pictures. A ready-made weekly PDF file is possible later (a scheduled job that saves the page as a PDF), but it adds moving parts, so only if people ask for it.
- **Track membership dues and donations without manual work.** Annual membership, with some members paying monthly. Research so far (details in the chat of 28 Nov):
  - **PayBox:** no public developer API, so the website can't read payments. A PayBox group does let its manager export who paid and how much to Excel, and run a new payment round each month in the same group. That's a monthly manual step, not automatic.
  - **Shul management systems built for this** (Gabay-net, GabbaiX, Kehila BeClick and others): a member list with debts, standing orders through the bank (Masav), card and Bit payments, Section 46 receipts, reminders by WhatsApp or email, and a personal area for each member. Gabay-net also connects to Nedarim Plus. These take the tracking off the gabbai completely; the website just links straight to their payment page.
  - **Donation and payment platforms with standing orders:** IsraelGives (cards, Bit, PayBox, Apple/Google Pay; monthly or yearly giving with automatic card replacement; automatic Section 46 receipts), JGive (standing orders, receipts, notices to other systems), Grow (PayBox, Bit and cards; standing orders by card; a notice after every payment), Nedarim Plus (common in shuls; developers use its hosted payment window).
  - **Questions for the board:** is the shul a registered amuta with Section 46 approval; who is treasurer; how members pay today; which fees are acceptable; and whether monthly payers will move from PayBox to a standing order, which is where the steady income comes from.
  - **Rules either way:** members' payment details stay with the provider, not in the website or its database; the "Pay membership" button and QR go straight to the payment, with the fewest taps.
  - **The board's answers (28 Nov):** no fees at all; members pay through PayBox; not an amuta with Section 46 approval yet. So the paid platforms and paid shul systems are out for now.
  - **Free path, no build needed:** a dedicated private PayBox group for membership. It offers up to four payment options (for example annual and monthly), hidden members and amounts, a new round each month that shows who hasn't paid, reminders to everyone unpaid in one tap or on a repeating schedule, marking cash payments, and an Excel export by email. PayBox has no automatic monthly charge, so members still tap Pay each month, but the reminders do the chasing.
  - **Limits to check before moving dues there:** since October 2025, PayBox's free Basic plan receives up to ₪50,000 a year and holds at most ₪20,000 at a time; the Plus plan (free with PayBox's own credit card) receives up to ₪100,000 a year. Check which account the shul's group pays into, and that dues plus donations stay under the cap.
  - **Optional small build later (free):** an admin-page tool where the treasurer drops in the PayBox Excel exports and sees the year's status per member, worked out in the browser and not saved on the site.
  - **Later:** once the shul has Section 46 approval, the receipt-issuing platforms become worth a look.
- **Admin sign-in lower on the website.** Move the board sign-in button from the top of the website to the bottom, out of the prime spot.
- **Facebook and Twitter links.** Links to the shul's Facebook and Twitter pages on the website, alongside the contact details.
- **Kiddush sponsorship line only when there is a Kiddush.** Today the website's Shabbat box shows "This week's Kiddush is still open for sponsorship" every week that has no Kiddush sponsor entered, whether or not a Kiddush is planned. Add a setting for whether there is a Kiddush this Shabbat (for example a "Kiddush this Shabbat" tick in the admin page, or a standing weekly Kiddush with weeks marked off), and show the invitation only when a Kiddush is planned and still has no sponsor.
- **Visitor analytics.** Count visits to the website (pages, phone vs computer, busiest days) and which admin sections get used, without cookies or personal data, so no consent banner is needed. A cookieless option such as Cloudflare Web Analytics fits (the domain is already at Cloudflare); it needs the page's security policy to allow its script, and the TV screen should be left out so it doesn't count as a visitor all day.
- **Welcome message for new admins.** After an owner adds an admin, the page offers to send them a ready-made welcome by email or WhatsApp: the link to the admin page and how to sign in with their Google account. It goes out from the owner's own account; nothing is sent automatically.

## Clean-up
- **Remove the retired v1/v2 code from the engine.** v1 and v2 are offline (their addresses open v3), but app.js still carries the old v1 home and TV drawing code and styles.css. Removing it makes the engine smaller.
- **One permanent address, with the version kept behind the scenes.** The current version is served at siachhasadeh.org/ itself: the website at /, the TV at /#display, the admin page at /#manage. No version number appears in any address again, so the TVs never need re-pointing after a release. Old addresses (/v3/, /v2/) keep working by forwarding to the root with the same # part. The version is tracked for us instead: a version number in one file, shown small at the bottom of the admin page, a git tag and stable branch for each release, and LEARNINGS.md. The TVs can be re-pointed to https://siachhasadeh.org/#display at the next visit already, because that address forwards to the current board today and will simply become it in v4. Check that the shul's github.io copy works at its own address after the move.
