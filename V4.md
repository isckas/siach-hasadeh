# Siach HaSadeh: v4 list

Things agreed for v4, after v3 is out. Nothing here is built yet.

## New features
- **Admin sign-in lower on the website.** Move the board sign-in button from the top of the website to the bottom, out of the prime spot.
- **Facebook and Twitter links.** Links to the shul's Facebook and Twitter pages on the website, alongside the contact details.
- **Welcome message for new admins.** After an owner adds an admin, the page offers to send them a ready-made welcome by email or WhatsApp: the link to the admin page and how to sign in with their Google account. It goes out from the owner's own account; nothing is sent automatically.

## Clean-up
- **Remove the retired v1/v2 code from the engine.** v1 and v2 are offline (their addresses open v3), but app.js still carries the old v1 home and TV drawing code and styles.css. Removing it makes the engine smaller.
- **Serve v3 at the root address** (siachhasadeh.org/ instead of /v3/), keeping /v3/ and the TV's /v3/#display working.

## Security follow-ups (each needs an OK, they change Firebase settings)
- **Spam protection, next step.** Finish the App Check setup once the website and TV have been checked (details are with the admins, not in this public file).
- **Remove the personal GitHub sign-in address** once the site fully moves to the shul's account.
- **Verify siachhasadeh.org in GitHub** (account Pages settings) so no one else can attach it to their own site; needs the owner signed in to GitHub.

## Needs the shul's GitHub account
- **Make the hourly copy retry.** About one hourly copy in three fails on GitHub's side ("No artifacts named github-pages were found"); the next hour usually works. Adding a retry to the shul repo's publish workflow would close the gap.

## Board decisions
- **Search engines.** For now the site is hidden from Google and other search engines (no-index on every page and a robots.txt). Decide later whether the main page should be findable; names in notices and the minutes PDF are why it's off.

## Decided, no change
- Any owner can remove another owner.
